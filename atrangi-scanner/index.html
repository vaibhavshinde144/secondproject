How Atrangi Scanner works

  1. Select a scan type.
  2. Take a photo or choose images. Every new page is checked for blur, exposure and contrast.
  3. Review Smart Clear, Original, Enhance, Clarity, Gray Scan or B&W Scan. Use Smart Crop or manual crop and rotate as required.
  4. Reorder or remove pages, then press Scan Complete. Normal capture controls are hidden after confirmation.
  5. After completion you can still Edit, Rotate, Remove, Add Page / Photo, Add Document Pages, or export to PDF, Word-compatible document or images.

Privacy: scanning and export are performed locally in your browser. The app has no server upload feature.

Camera: browser camera capture requires HTTPS on mobile. GitHub Pages provides HTTPS.
